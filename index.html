Exam Rules — CFA Level 1 (February 2027)

📋180 questions total across 2 sessions of 90 questions each
⏱135 minutes per session (2 hours 15 min); ~90 seconds per question
🔤A / B / C only — three answer choices per question (no D)
✖No negative marking — answer every question; guessing is rational
⚑Flag for review — mark questions to revisit before submitting

☕Optional break between sessions; timer stops, your time does not
🎯Pass band ~70% — exact MPS set post-exam by the CFA Institute
